Glucose Tracker

What it does

A simple, private blood-glucose log: record a reading with its context and a note, watch the trend over time, and get a nudge when the day's fasting reading hasn't been logged yet.

How it's built

A small self-hosted app backed by SQLite. A scheduled check each morning looks for a fasting reading and sends a gentle reminder if one's missing. The trend sparkline and history make patterns easy to spot. (The screenshot here is cropped to the entry form on purpose, the actual readings are private health data.)
