Qdrant Browser

What it does

A live window into the vector database that powers the email search, every collection, its size, and its health, browsable in the browser.

How it's built

Qdrant's own dashboard, running in the Kubernetes cluster. The main collection, email_index, holds 100,000+ points at 768 dimensions with cosine distance, the numeric fingerprints of every indexed email. Alongside it are demo and test collections and a small "memory" store.
