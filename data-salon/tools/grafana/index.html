Grafana Dashboards

What it does

The observability front door, live dashboards over every metric and log the machine collects, from CPU and disk to weather, network traffic, and the health of every service.

How it's built

Prometheus scrapes thirteen targets across the host, the lab services, and the Kubernetes cluster; Loki holds the logs; Grafana renders both and evaluates the alert rules. When a rule fires, it doesn't page anyone directly, it routes back through the relay and into the model, which decides whether and how to raise it. (Shown here at its login screen; the dashboards themselves stay behind auth.)
