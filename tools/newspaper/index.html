Daily Newspaper

What it does

A fresh newspaper every morning, written from scratch. Each edition gathers the day's most significant developments across global affairs, US national news, Brooklyn local, and technology, and lays them out like a real front page with links to the source articles.

How it's built

Every headline is handed to a named correspondent persona, a foreign-affairs writer, a metro reporter, a technology columnist. A multi-agent workflow then spawns one writer per headline; each does its own live web research and writes a full 400–600 word article in that reporter's voice. A digest of the morning's email is folded in, and the finished edition is built and published automatically.
